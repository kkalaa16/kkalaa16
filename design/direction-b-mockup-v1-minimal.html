  • Compared 2-blade vs. 3-blade Gemfan 6042 propeller configurations on the same rig and mesh strategy.

DESIGN.md: Direction B

Reading this as: solo-engineer portfolio for aerospace/propulsion/ML recruiters, where the differentiator is methodological rigor, not aesthetics borrowed from software-startup design. The identity is built from the actual instruments of the work: mesh plots, combustion diagrams, drag curves, captioned the way a real technical report captions its figures.

  • Fonts: system grotesk stack for display/body (heavy weight, tight tracking, uppercase display), system mono for data/captions. No serif anywhere. This is a technical-report register, not editorial.
  • Palette chosen to avoid the AI-default cream+brass "premium artisan" family: this paper reads cool grey-green, not warm cream, and the accent is a specific hazard red-orange, not clay or ochre.
  • Figure numbering (Fig. 01, Fig. 02…) is only used on real images being captioned, matching the actual convention of an engineering report, never as decorative section numbers.
  • Registration-mark corner ticks are a genuine drafting/print-registration convention, used sparingly to frame the cover sheet only.
  • Single light "documentation paper" substrate by design. A printed report doesn't ship a dark-mode edition either.
  • Contrast: ink-dim at 7.1:1 against the paper background, comfortably above AA for body text.
  • Intro gate becomes a cover sheet: capability, credential, tools, "Open the file." Same content strategy as Direction A, different register.
  • Full archive stays open by default on the real page; skills sit above any visualization; GRE/TOEFL removed from Achievements.
